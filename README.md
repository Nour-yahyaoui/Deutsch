# 🛂 Sprachpass — جواز اللغة

A mobile-first, offline-friendly web app for learning **A2-level German** through Arabic — with vocabulary, quizzes, fill-in-the-blank sentences, two-person dialogues, a daily challenge, and a progress dashboard. No backend, no build step: just an `index.html` and a `data.json`.

## ✨ Features

- **Courses & lessons** — vocabulary organized by topic (greetings, numbers, family, food, daily verbs, home & city, weather, shopping, and more), each word shown with its Arabic meaning and Arabic-script pronunciation
- **Learn tracking** — tap a word to mark it learned; progress is saved locally and earns XP
- **Quiz mode** — multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions generated *only* from words you've marked as learned
- **Sentences & dialogues** — short German sentences and two-person conversations built around your learned vocabulary, with letter-by-letter hints that get easier or harder based on your level
- **Daily challenge** — one bonus quiz per day that feeds a streak counter
- **Dashboard** — XP, streak, per-course progress, quiz accuracy, and recently learned words
- **Responsive** — bottom tab navigation on mobile, sidebar navigation on desktop
- **100% local** — all progress is stored in the browser's `localStorage`; nothing is sent anywhere
